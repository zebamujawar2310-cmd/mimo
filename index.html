Overview

What a Personal Consultation Actually Involves

A consultation is a structured conversation. Your birth details are interpreted with care, explained in plain language, and reflected back to you as guidance rather than fixed prediction.

Astrology & numerology, explained

Astrology works with the position of the sky at the moment of your birth. The date, time and place of birth are used to prepare your chart, which is then read as a symbolic map of tendencies, influences and timing.

Numerology works with your date of birth and your name, reducing both to core numbers that describe patterns in temperament, motivation and expression.

  • How birth details are used

    Date, time and place are used only to prepare your chart and calculate your numbers. More accurate details produce a more precise reading.

  • What to expect in the session

    A one-to-one discussion where each area of your chart is explained, questions are welcome, and the themes discussed are summarised at the end.

  • Guidance, not certainty

    You receive an interpretation and a set of considerations to reflect upon. Decisions remain entirely yours, and no outcome is promised.
